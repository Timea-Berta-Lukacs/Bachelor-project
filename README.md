Operating steps:
	1.	Each Arduino board is connected individually to its power source, namely the external battery.
	2.	Once the Arduino boards are powered, the vehicle enters its default operating mode, namely the “movement” mode, indicated by the green LED turning on on the remote control device.
	3.	The remote control device is correctly positioned in the hands, with the tilt sensor placed to the left and facing forward.
	4.	To move the vehicle forward, the device is slightly tilted forward, and to move backward it is tilted slightly backward. For right or left movement, the remote control device is similarly tilted to the right or left.
	5.	Once the vehicle is positioned as desired, the system can switch to the second operating mode, namely the “gripping” mode, by pressing the button located on the control device. The transition to this mode is indicated by the LED turning off, and at this moment the servo motors controlling the robotic arm are ready.
	6.	By tilting the hand forward and to the right, the robotic arm moves forward; conversely, tilting the hand backward and to the left causes the arm to move backward.
	7.	After positioning the robotic arm as desired, by bending the sensor the gripper of the robotic arm closes; conversely, by extending it, the gripper opens.
	8.	Switching back to the first operating mode is performed by pressing the button.
